OUR STORY

Tradition, with
a fresh point of view.

Zubi's began with a simple belief: familiar flavours deserve to be made with the same care they receive at home. Every jar, blend and bite is a celebration of Kerala's generous, vibrant food culture.

We pair recipes inspired by tradition with thoughtful modern presentation - so sharing a taste of home feels easy, wherever you are.

asked questions.

How do I place an order?+

Add your favourites to the bag, enter your delivery details, then send the pre-filled order to Zubi's on WhatsApp. The team will confirm availability and delivery.

How should I store my pickles?+

For the best experience, follow the storage instructions on the product label and always use a clean, dry spoon.

Do you offer different pack sizes?+

Available formats can vary by product. Select a product in the shop to see its current pack-size options.

Where can I find Zubi's?+

You can visit the Zubi's store or reach out on WhatsApp for help with product availability and delivery.
