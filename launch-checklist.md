# Zubi's launch checklist

## Built into this storefront

- [x] Brand-aligned forest green, sage, cream, and gold palette; consistent typography and custom favicon.
- [x] Sticky navigation with Home, Shop, Our Story, Our Craft, FAQ, and Contact.
- [x] Homepage hero, clear calls to action, featured shop grid, brand story, process, FAQ, newsletter, contact links, and social link.
- [x] Responsive product catalogue with product name, description, price, category, search, filters, sorting, and add-to-cart.
- [x] Cart with per-product quantity controls, removal, subtotal, total quantity, and grand total.
- [x] Local-storage cart persistence across refreshes.
- [x] WhatsApp order checkout with required name, phone, address, optional notes, validation, confirmation dialog, and URL-encoded order message for `9846525325`.
- [x] Mobile-friendly layout, touch-sized controls, reduced animation, semantic HTML, keyboard-focused controls, labels, skip link, and strong contrast.
- [x] SEO foundation: title, description, Open Graph tags, structured data, favicon, `robots.txt`, and sitemap template.
- [x] Footer with social, contact, FAQ, privacy, and shipping-policy links.
- [x] Floating WhatsApp support button and an on-page customer-review placeholder.

## Required before public launch

- [ ] Replace illustrative CSS product visuals and stock section images with approved Zubi's logo, product packshots, and branded photography. Add accurate alt text for every final image.
- [ ] Replace demonstration prices, descriptions, pack sizes, ingredient/allergen data, stock status, delivery timings, and policies with approved business information.
- [ ] Add verified customer reviews, ratings, certifications, awards, email address, phone number, business hours, and any return/refund policy that applies.
- [ ] Publish the site on HTTPS, replace the example sitemap domain with the real domain, and connect Google Search Console.
- [ ] Connect a real newsletter provider, form spam protection, analytics/consent banner, Google Analytics, and Meta Pixel only if used.
- [ ] If customer accounts, online payments, or a live admin dashboard are later needed, add a secure hosted commerce/backend system for product, inventory, order, customer, and discount management.
- [ ] Configure image compression, caching/CDN, backups, privacy compliance, and a custom 404 page in the selected host.
- [ ] Test the final hosted website on Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox, Android, and iPhone; verify all links, WhatsApp flow, product search, cart persistence, form validation, accessibility, speed, and SEO metadata.

## Intentional scope

This site does not take online payments or automatically reserve inventory. The Zubi's team confirms every order, availability, delivery cost, and final total over WhatsApp, as requested.
